# Normandy, 6 June 1944 — 5 s woodcut (style: Woodcut Print)

No voice, no subtitles beyond one letterpress margin caption. Synthesised sound only (no downloads).

## Three candidate structures
1. **Three plates, one landing**: wide sea/ramp -> beach obstacles + figures -> one helmet in the surf. Wordless-novel sequencing.
2. **One pull-back**: start on a single carved ramp, pull back as cuts spread until the whole beach appears.
3. **Block then print**: show the inked block, carve a wave, pull the print.

**Chosen: 1.** 5 s is too short for a pull-back to land; three self-contained plates on hard cuts (each a new impression) read clearly at thumbnail size and match the style's wordless-novel grammar.

## Logline / arc
Boats come in under a sky of rays; men wade through iron obstacles; one shell flash burns orange, then only a helmet is left in the surf. setup -> turn (flash) -> aftermath.

## Benchmark
Learn: Masereel/Ward plate sequencing, Kollwitz emotional direction of the gouge. Don't take: any actual plate, figure or border.

## Sound design

## Type
