# Cheese, Please! (PS1 edition) — 30 s, 1990s 3D game look

Same story, timing, cast and voice lines as the rubber-hose version (Pip the mouse steals cheese, Boris the cat chases, slips, the arm-knot gag, iris out). The look is rebuilt as a mid-90s console 3D game. This is **not** a library style: the renderer is written for this film on the library's three.js/Chromium capture tools.

PS1 look kit: render at 360x270 and upscale 4x with hard pixels (4:3 gate); vertex positions snap to the pixel grid (polygon wobble); affine (non-perspective-correct) texture mapping; Gouraud vertex lighting; 64x64 nearest-filtered textures; 15-bit colour with 4x4 ordered dither; depth fog; characters animated at 15 fps; 8-bit game voice clips (11 kHz, 8-bit); chiptune score (2 pulse + triangle + noise); scanline/CRT overlay; pixel-font HUD, title screen and "PRESS START" end screen.

Cue map: unchanged — 120 BPM, cuts at 6, 8, 15, 19, 27; music stops (digital silence) at 6.0-6.5, 14.0-14.5, 24.0-24.25.
