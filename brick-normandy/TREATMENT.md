# Normandy in bricks — 12 s, Brick Toy style (Lemo-Opuscar)

A brick world on a real surface (walnut desk), shot like macro toy photography: studio HDRI + softboxes, GTAO, physical depth of field, 2x-style finish at 1x; bricks and figures step on twos (12 fps), the camera moves smoothly on ones. Characters are built from bricks (round-brick helmets, 2x2 torsos, round-brick arms): no minifigure silhouette, no brand marks. Native moves used: "toy on a real desk" and the scale reveal; "break without harm" for the fallen figures (bricks come apart and lie scattered; no injury detail). Red is red toy pieces.

Structure chosen (of three): a crossing — S1 the line of figures marches up out of the surf toward camera (0-3.6 s); S2 macro close-up of the lead figure kneeling with his mouth open, red pieces on his arms, a small brick burst goes up behind him (3.6-8.0 s); S3 he stands and marches on while the camera cranes up and back until the whole beach is a baseplate on a desk, then fade (8.0-12 s).
Narration (solemn, three lines, reused from the previous cut): n1 at 0.4 s, n2 at 3.9 s, silence 7.9-8.5 s, n3 at 8.6 s.
Sound: music-box melody + low drone, heartbeat 3.9-7.9 s, muted boom and clacking bricks at the burst, brick footsteps, wind/room bed.
