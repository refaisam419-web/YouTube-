# Cheese, Please! — 30 s rubber-hose chase (style: 1930s Rubber Hose Cartoon)

Original characters: **Boris**, a big sleepy grey house cat, and **Pip**, a tiny white mouse. No existing cartoon character, name or design is used. Strictly greyscale, 4:3 gate, 120 BPM (beat = 12 frames, so every beat lands on a drawing of the twos).

## Three candidate structures
1. **Three-gag chase**: steal -> chase + pratfall -> a stretching-arm trap that backfires.
2. **Talent show**: kitchen objects perform; the mouse wins the cheese. (Too many characters for 30 s.)
3. **Singalong**: a bouncing ball over a cheese jingle. (Strong for music, weak for a cat-and-mouse story.)

**Chosen: 1.** It is the only one where the two leads carry the whole film, and each of its three gags uses a native move: rubber-hose stretch, Mickey-mousing the chase, the film as a physical object (iris grabbed, card pulled).

## Logline / arc
A mouse tiptoes out for one wedge of cheese and wakes the cat; the chase ends in a pratfall; the cat's long arm reaches into the mousehole and ties itself in a knot; the mouse eats the cheese and pulls the end card over the cat.
setup (0-8 s) -> chase (8-19 s) -> trap backfires (19-27 s) -> iris out (27-30 s)

## Benchmark (grammar only)
Early-1930s sound cartoons: every step a note, chase on a windmill-leg run, a stop (band silence) before the pratfall. Not taking any character, melody or gag from them.

## Voices (Kokoro, English; radio chain HP 260 / LP 4600 Hz, tanh saturation)
Pip: high, quick, whispered then sassy. Boris: low, slow, grumbly. Four to five short lines, merged into one title card where they run together.

## Title cards
IM Fell English SC on a black plate with a white double rule; Shrikhand fat title. Subtitle cards hold >= max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s).

## Cue map
120 BPM, beats at 0.5 s. Cuts at 6.0, 8.0, 15.0, 19.0, 27.0 (all on the bar). Band STOP at 6.0-6.4 (before "Breakfast!"), 14.0-14.5 (before the pratfall CLANG at 14.5), 24.0-24.25 (before the snap-back).

## Sound design
Ambience: projector gate clatter + hiss bed (the only sound allowed inside a stop). Foley: woodblock steps, slide whistle for every stretch/fall, "boing" spring, pot clang (inharmonic bell partials), rubber creak on the arm stretch. Music: stride piano, tuba, clarinet, banjo, brushes, all synthesised; optical-soundtrack ageing pass.
