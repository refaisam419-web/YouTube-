# D-Day in bricks — 10 s (style: Brick Toy), English narration

Respectful, no weapons fired: the story is boats, a beach and a held pose.

## Three candidate structures
1. **Crossing**: boat slides across a paper "sea" to a tan baseplate beach, figures wade up it.
2. **Catalogue**: three brick builds (boat, obstacle, flag), one per beat.
3. **Scale reveal**: start macro on one boat, pull back to the whole desk.

**Chosen: 1 + a scale-reveal ending.** The crossing is readable in 10 s; the pull-back at the end (STYLE native move) gives the turn: the whole beach is a toy on a desk.

## Logline / arc
A little brick boat crosses to a beach; a brick figure plants a flag; the camera pulls back and it is all a desk. setup -> arrival -> reveal.

## Benchmark (grammar only)
Macro toy photography; stop-motion on twos with smooth camera. Not taking any brick-film's characters, sets or music.

## Narration (Kokoro, ~10 s total)
1. "June sixth, nineteen forty-four."  (0.4-2.4 s)
2. "A thousand boats crossed the sea to one beach." (3.0-6.4 s)
3. "And the world turned." (7.2-8.9 s)

## Shot list / beats (24 fps camera, 12 fps bricks)
| t | shot | camera | sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3.0 | S1 low macro on a grey brick landing boat on a blue paper sea, wooden desk blurred behind; boat glides toward beach | tracking alongside, low angle | room tone, soft wave noise, glockenspiel drone |
| 3.0-6.6 | S2 medium: boat grounds on the tan baseplate beach, ramp drops (brick click), 3 brick figures hop out and walk up past brick obstacles | slow push, focus on lead figure's head | plastic footsteps, click on ramp, pizzicato |
| 6.6-7.2 | silence beat | held | music drops to room tone |
| 7.2-10 | S3 lead figure plants a small flag; camera pulls back/up to a high wide: whole beach on the desk beside a mug | crane up (scale reveal) | glockenspiel + melodica final chord |

## Sound design
Ambience: room tone + waves 0-3. Foley: brick clicks on every snap, tiny footsteps, wood clack. Music: glockenspiel, pizzicato, melodica (synthesised). Silence 6.6-7.2.

## Subtitles
Dark rounded pill low in frame, Fredoka, small brick icon, narration lines.
