# Cheese, Please! (3D cartoon edition) — 30 s, modern CG-cartoon look

Same story, cast, timing and voice lines as the other editions (Pip the mouse steals the cheese; Boris the cat chases, slips, the arm-knot gag, iris out). Look: warm, soft-lit, rounded CG feature-animation style; smooth movement.

- 16:9, 1920x1080, 24 fps. Smooth shading, soft shadows, studio-HDRI reflections, rim light.
- Characters: rounded glossy bodies, big eyes with highlights and eyelids, rubber-hose two-segment limbs with a bend.
- Smooth motion: poses are sampled continuously at 24 fps (no stepping) and temporally filtered (+-0.1 s) so the story's key poses blend instead of popping; turn-arounds sweep through the camera; stretch on vertical speed; tail and ears follow with lag.
- Camera: same shot plan (stage-wide, close-up, trucking pan, mediums, crane/iris), with gentle drift.
- Sound: bright orchestral-cartoon score (pizzicato, clarinet, piano, tuba, banjo, brushes, xylophone-like), clean voices, same cue map and band stops.
